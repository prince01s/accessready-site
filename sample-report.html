What this snapshot does not cover

Automated tools find only part of WCAG failures. Keyboard-only navigation, screen-reader testing (NVDA/VoiceOver), patient portals and scheduling tools from vendors, videos and mobile apps all need manual review, plus documentation of results and remediation dates — which is what the HHS guidance and health center associations recommend keeping on file.
